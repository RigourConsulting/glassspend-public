Release Notes and Version History

Log of Application Milestones, Enhancements, and Documented Boundaries

Version 0.1.0 (October 2026) (Internal Testing Milestone)

This release establishes the core GlassSpend personal cost intelligence platform for Australian households preparing for controlled testing.

Key features included

  • On-device ML Kit OCR: Local Latin text recognition for bill photos and scanned PDFs with zero image transmission off-device.
  • Australian date presentation: Comprehensive enforcement of DD/MM/YYYY across all screens, due date schedules, activity logs, and candidate confirmation forms.
  • Explain My Bill Change: Deterministic decomposition of consecutive bills into consumption shifts, tariff rate rises, supply charges, and expired discounts.
  • Stay & Save factual negotiation: Generation of factual retention negotiation scripts for existing provider discussions without automatic switching.
  • Interactive Calendar & Reminders: Month grid view and agenda list with exact AlarmManager notifications for upcoming payment due dates.
  • Responsive Accessibility: Dynamic action button stacking at 150% and 200% font scale, WCAG AA/AAA contrast ratios, and TalkBack semantics.
  • Google Play Billing & Cryptographic Verification: Client billing integration paired with server-side RSA signature verification and token replay protection.
  • AER reference data pilot: Ingestion of official public reference tariffs from the Australian Energy Regulator for NEM states.

Known boundaries and limitations

  • Western Australia and Northern Territory: Regulated by separate state frameworks (ERA WA and Utilities Commission NT). Live monitoring feeds are not available; manual tracking is fully supported.
  • Water and council rates: As statutory monopoly supplies without competitive retail markets, GlassSpend provides bill tracking and due date reminders without supplier switching recommendations.
  • Automated switching: By design, GlassSpend does not execute automated contract switching or handle customer bill payments.
