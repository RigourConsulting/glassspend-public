Version 1.0.0 (October 2026) — Initial Acceptance Milestone

This major release establishes the core GlassSpend personal cost intelligence platform for Australian households.

Key Features Included

  • On-Device ML Kit OCR: Bounded, local Latin text recognition for bill photos and scanned PDFs with zero image transmission off-device.
  • Australian Date Presentation: Comprehensive enforcement of DD/MM/YYYY across all screens, due date schedules, activity logs, and candidate confirmation forms.
  • Explain My Bill Change: Deterministic decomposition of consecutive bills into consumption shifts, tariff rate rises, supply charges, and expired discounts.
  • Stay & Save Factual Negotiation: Generation of factual retention negotiation scripts for existing provider discussions without automatic switching.
  • Act Before Deadline: Multi-tiered reminder escalation (30d, 14d, 7d, 1d, 0d) for contract renewals and benefit period expiries.
  • Truthful Monitoring Attribution: Distinction between active live monitoring and local tracked items, with explicit disclosure of synthetic demonstration records.
  • AER Reference Data Pilot: Ingestion of official public reference tariffs from the Australian Energy Regulator for NEM states.

Known Boundaries & Limitations

  • Western Australia & Northern Territory: Regulated by separate state frameworks (ERA WA and Utilities Commission NT). Live monitoring feeds are not available; manual tracking is fully supported.
  • Water & Council Rates: As statutory monopoly supplies without competitive retail markets, GlassSpend provides bill tracking and due date reminders without supplier switching recommendations.
  • Automated Switching: By design, GlassSpend does not execute automated contract switching or handle customer bill payments.
