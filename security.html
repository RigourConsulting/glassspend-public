Security Architecture & Threat Model

Zero Credential Collection, On-Device Intelligence, and Cryptographic Shredding

1. The Zero-Credential Architecture

The core tenet of GlassSpend is that your external accounts remain private and separate:

  • No bank logins: We do not ask for netbanking usernames, passwords, or SMS verification codes.
  • No utility portal passwords: You never connect or store online utility account passwords in GlassSpend.
  • Zero Credential Retention: Because GlassSpend does not request or store third-party credentials, your external utility and banking accounts remain completely independent.

2. On-Device Document Recognition

When you scan a paper bill or import an image, processing occurs entirely within the sandboxed environment on your Android device using Google ML Kit. The pixels of your bill scans do not leave your phone, completely eliminating cloud document interception risks.

3. Data Protection in Transit and at Rest

  • In Transit: All communication between the GlassSpend Android client and our backend APIs is strictly enforced via TLS 1.3 with DNS pinning and strict certificate validation. Cleartext HTTP is disallowed at the operating system network security configuration level.
  • At Rest: On Android, local databases and temporary scan files are isolated within Android's sandboxed private app storage. No raw bill documents or images are transmitted to external cloud servers in on-device capture mode.
  • Cryptographic Shredding: Prior to file deletion, temporary files are overwritten with zeros followed by cryptographically secure pseudo-random bytes to prevent data remanence on flash storage.

4. Ephemeral Lifespan & Local Storage Shredding

Under on-device capture mode, scan images and decoded bitmaps are retained only during the immediate extraction session. Temporary cache files are permanently overwritten with random bytes and shredded upon confirmation or cancellation.
