Support & FAQ

Help, troubleshooting, and supported household services

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which bill formats and categories does GlassSpend support?

GlassSpend supports scanned or photographed images (JPEG, PNG, WebP) and PDF invoices for:

  • Electricity & Natural Gas: Retail plans across NSW, QLD, SA, ACT, and TAS.
  • Broadband & NBN: Monthly internet statements, speed tiers, and bundled services.
  • Mobile: Postpaid and recurring SIM-only plans from major Australian telcos.
  • Streaming & Subscriptions: Monthly entertainment and cloud subscription invoices.
  • Council Rates & Water: Due date and payment tracking (statutory monopoly supply).
  • Purchase Receipts: Replenishable household goods and grocery items.

2. Why didn't OCR detect my plan name automatically?

Many Australian retail bills print the retailer's commercial marketing plan name on the second page or within small summary tables. When an exact plan name cannot be determined with complete confidence, GlassSpend honestly marks it as Unresolved Plan and prompts you to select or confirm it manually rather than guessing.

3. Why does my energy watch say "Tracking" instead of "Live Monitoring"?

In accordance with our Truth in Presentation policy, GlassSpend only displays "Live Monitoring" when an active, continuous background worker has verified connectivity with an official public regulatory data feed. For manually synced accounts or regional jurisdictions outside the National Electricity Market (such as WA or NT), records are accurately labelled as Tracked items.

4. Will GlassSpend switch my electricity or broadband provider automatically?

No, never. GlassSpend is strictly an independent cost intelligence and analysis tool. We generate factual negotiation scripts ("Stay & Save") and provide official public plan references, but you retain 100% control over any contact or contracts with providers.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

  • Document scanning tips: Ensure good overhead lighting and avoid heavy shadows when photographing paper bills. For multi-page PDF statements, the first two pages usually contain the key tariff blocks.
  • Notifications not firing: Ensure battery optimisation is disabled for GlassSpend in Android Settings, allowing exact alarm delivery for bill due dates.
