Support and FAQ

Help, troubleshooting, and supported household services

Frequently asked questions

1. Which bill formats and categories does GlassSpend support?

GlassSpend supports photographs, image files (JPEG, PNG, WebP), and PDF digital invoices across:

  • Electricity and natural gas: Retail plans and household accounts across NSW, QLD, SA, VIC, ACT, TAS, and WA (including WA retail natural gas plans and Synergy electricity tracking). In the Northern Territory, electricity accounts (Jacana Energy) and bottled LPG gas supplies are supported.
  • Home internet and NBN: Monthly fixed broadband invoices, speed tiers, and bundled services.
  • Mobile services: Postpaid and recurring SIM-only statements from Australian providers.
  • Streaming and digital subscriptions: Monthly entertainment and cloud subscription statements.
  • Council rates and water: Due date reminders and payment tracking for statutory utility supplies.
  • Purchase receipts: Replenishable household goods and grocery receipts.

2. Why was a field marked for review during bill scanning?

Many Australian retail invoices present complex tables or print the plan name on secondary pages. When optical character recognition detects multiple possible numbers (for example, distinguishing between previous balance, new charges, and amount due), GlassSpend honestly flags the field for your review rather than guessing.

3. Will GlassSpend switch my electricity or broadband provider automatically?

No, never. GlassSpend is strictly an independent cost organisation and analysis tool. We generate factual negotiation scripts ("Stay & Save") and provide official public plan benchmarks, but you retain full personal control over your contracts and provider relationships.

4. How do monthly equivalent calculations work?

Recurring expenses are converted to consistent monthly equivalent amounts using deterministic arithmetic in integer minor units (cents):

  • Weekly bills are multiplied by 52 and divided by 12.
  • Fortnightly bills are multiplied by 26 and divided by 12.
  • Quarterly bills are divided by 3.
  • Annual bills are divided by 12.

5. How do I use the Calendar schedule?

The Calendar screen presents an interactive monthly grid with coloured dots for payment obligations and personal notes. Tapping any date reveals scheduled bills and allows you to attach custom household reminders.

Troubleshooting common questions

  • Camera capture clarity: Ensure adequate lighting and hold your smartphone steady over the paper bill. Capture the full account summary table containing the provider logo, due date, and total amount due.
  • Notification delivery: To ensure due date reminders sound reliably, verify that battery optimisation for GlassSpend is set to unrestricted or standard in Android system settings.
  • Missing browser apps: If tapping a legal or support link does not open a page, ensure that an approved web browser (such as Chrome, Samsung Internet, or Firefox) is installed and enabled on your device.
