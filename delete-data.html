Account and Data Deletion Request

Google Play Data Safety Disclosure and Household Data Deletion

1. Our deletion commitment

GlassSpend is engineered with local-first privacy autonomy. You have the full right to delete any individual bill, purchase receipt, service watch, or your complete data store at any time. When you request data deletion, all associated local records are permanently wiped.

2. Local-first architecture (no external account required)

GlassSpend does not require you to create an external consumer account, password, or third-party cloud login. Your records are stored in a sandboxed SQLite database on your device. Consequently, deleting your data does not require logging into an external web portal.

3. How to delete data within the Android application

You can execute an immediate local wipe directly inside the GlassSpend Android app:

  1. Open the GlassSpend app on your Android smartphone or tablet.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Data Management card.
  4. Tap Clear All Local Data.
  5. Review the confirmation prompt and tap Clear Data.

Immediate effect: Your local SQLite database is cleared and recreated, all active reminders and alarms are cancelled via Android AlarmManager, and all temporary capture files are overwritten and unlinked.

4. Web deletion request (without app access)

If you have uninstalled the application and wish to request deletion of any server-side diagnostic records, installation session keys, or subscription linkage records, contact our privacy team:

Email: rigour.consulting@gmail.com
Location: Rigour Consulting Pty Ltd, Perth WA, Australia
Subject: GlassSpend Data Deletion Request
Details to provide: Your Google Play purchase order number (GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX) or your device installation identifier if known.
Processing: Requests are processed within 2 business days and confirmed via email.

5. Important note regarding subscriptions

Clearing your local records or uninstalling the GlassSpend application does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. To stop recurring subscription renewals, you must cancel your subscription through Google Play (see Subscription Help).

6. Data retention disclosures

  • Raw bill images: Processed on-device and never stored on remote servers. Temporary capture files in cache are deleted immediately upon review confirmation.
  • Subscription and financial records: Google Play transaction tokens and payment verification records are retained solely as required by Australian taxation and statutory record-keeping obligations (5-year statutory retention under Australian taxation law).
