Review every extracted field before saving. You stay in control of your numbers, confirm providers, verify due dates, and correct ongoing service rates.

Factual Negotiation Scripts

Supported household categories

GlassSpend is designed for everyday recurring household expenses. We support tracking and organising commitments across:

  • Mobile services: Postpaid and recurring SIM-only plans across major Australian telecommunications providers.
  • Home internet and NBN: Fixed broadband statements, speed tiers, and bundled household connections.
  • Electricity and natural gas: Retail energy accounts across National Electricity Market states (NSW, QLD, SA, VIC, ACT, and TAS).
  • Water and council rates: Due date reminders and payment tracking for statutory utility supplies.
  • Recurring subscriptions: Monthly streaming services, software licences, fitness memberships, and digital subscriptions.
  • Replenishable household goods: Tracking staple consumable expenses and purchase receipts.

Impartial cost intelligence

GlassSpend provides objective, mathematical breakdowns so you can assess your household commitments with clarity.

We do not accept retailer sales commissions, we do not switch your provider automatically, and we never request your online banking credentials or utility account passwords.

Independent arithmetic: Comparison figures are computed down to the cent using published reference data from official regulatory bodies such as the Australian Energy Regulator. Bill tracking remains distinct from supplier-offer intelligence.
