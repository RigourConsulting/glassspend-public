Subscription Management & Help

Google Play Subscriptions, Tier Comparison, Cancellation, and Refund Policy

1. Subscription Tiers Overview

GlassSpend provides a generous Free tier for basic household bill tracking, alongside an optional GlassSpend Plus subscription for advanced analytics and continuous monitoring:

  • GlassSpend Free (A$0.00): Track up to 3 active household services, scan up to 5 bills per month, access basic due date reminders, and export full data at any time.
  • GlassSpend Plus (Proposed candidate pricing: A$9.99/mo or A$79.99/yr, subject to finalisation before commercial launch): Unlimited active services, unlimited on-device document scans, full historical bill change disaggregation (Explain My Bill Change), Stay & Save factual negotiation scripts, and automated AER regulatory price monitoring.

2. How to Cancel Your Subscription via Google Play

All GlassSpend subscriptions are processed and managed through Google Play. You can cancel at any time directly through your Android device:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android smartphone or tablet.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then select Subscriptions.
  4. Select GlassSpend from your list of active subscriptions.
  5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the on-screen prompts.

Retained Access: When you cancel, you will continue to have full access to GlassSpend Plus features until the end of your current paid billing period. After this date, your account automatically reverts to the Free tier without deleting any of your existing bill records.

3. Refund Policy

Since subscription billing is processed directly by Google Play:

  • Within 48 hours of purchase: You can request a refund directly through the Google Play Store under Google's standard refund policy. Visit the Google Play Refund Request page.
  • After 48 hours: If you experience technical faults or service interruptions that our team cannot resolve, please contact our support team at support@rigourconsulting.com.au with your Google Play order number (format: GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX). We comply fully with statutory refund rights under Australian Consumer Law.

4. The "Never Paywalled" Guarantee

We believe your household records belong to you. We promise that you will never be paywalled from:

  • Viewing or exporting your own entered bills and extracted figures.
  • Deleting your account and wiping all stored data.
  • Setting at least one basic reminder for any tracked bill due date.
