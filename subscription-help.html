Subscription Management and Help

Google Play Subscriptions, Tier Comparison, Cancellation, and Refund Policy

1. Membership tiers overview

GlassSpend provides a functional Free tier for basic household bill tracking, alongside an optional GlassSpend Pro subscription for advanced analytics and continuous monitoring:

  • GlassSpend Free (A$0.00): Track up to 3 active household services, scan documents on-device, access due date reminders, manage personal calendar notes, and export full data at any time.
  • GlassSpend Pro (A$9.99 per month or A$79.99 per year, GST-inclusive): Unlimited active monitored recurring services, unlimited bill occurrences, detailed historical bill comparisons, spending trends by provider, category, and period, enhanced price-change insights, multiple configurable reminders per service, and comprehensive spending summaries. Annual plan saves A$39.89 per year (approximately 33.27% savings, equivalent to approximately A$6.67 per month).

2. How to cancel your subscription via Google Play

All GlassSpend subscriptions are processed and managed through Google Play. You can cancel at any time directly through your Android device:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android smartphone or tablet.
  2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then select Subscriptions.
  4. Select GlassSpend from your list of active subscriptions.
  5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the on-screen prompts.

Retained access until period ends: When you cancel, you continue to have full access to GlassSpend Pro features until the conclusion of your current paid billing period. After this date, your app automatically reverts to the Free tier without deleting or locking any of your existing bill records.

3. Refund policy

Because subscription transactions are processed directly by Google Play:

  • Within 48 hours of purchase: You can request a refund directly through Google Play under Google's standard refund policy. Visit the Google Play Refund Request page.
  • After 48 hours: If you experience technical faults or service issues, contact our support team at rigour.consulting@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (format: GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX). We comply fully with statutory refund rights under Australian Consumer Law.

4. The "Never Paywalled" guarantee

Your household financial records belong to you. We promise that you will never be paywalled from:

  • Viewing or exporting your own entered bills and confirmed records.
  • Clearing your local records and wiping all stored data.
  • Setting basic due date reminders for tracked bills.
  • Accessing your historical information following plan downgrade.
