How Comparisons Work

Deterministic Mathematics, Regulatory Benchmarks, and Complete Commercial Independence

1. Our commercial independence

GlassSpend is funded through user subscriptions, not retailer commissions or referral fees. We do not sell leads, we do not accept switching kickbacks, and we do not rank service providers based on commercial relationships.

GlassSpend takes zero retailer commissions. All plan comparisons and benchmark assessments are independent, transparent, and aligned solely with consumer interests.

2. Pure deterministic mathematics

All calculations in GlassSpend are governed by reproducible, deterministic arithmetic. We never use generative models to guess prices, round numbers unpredictably, or fabricate missing values:

  • Integer minor units: All currency amounts are handled in integer cents (for example, 2,450 cents instead of floating-point $24.50), eliminating binary rounding errors.
  • Exact unit rates: Energy comparisons compute exact daily supply charges multiplied by calendar days plus tiered usage rates (cents per kWh) based on your real meter history.
  • No synthetic extrapolation: If your bill is missing usage data or meter readings, the comparison engine explicitly reports that more information is required rather than guessing.

3. Official regulatory source data

GlassSpend benchmarks retail energy offers against official public data from the Australian Energy Regulator (AER), the Default Market Offer (DMO) / Victorian Default Offer (VDO) reference benchmarks, and state utility gazettes.

  • National Electricity Market (NEM) jurisdictions: New South Wales (NSW), South East Queensland (QLD), South Australia (SA), Victoria (VIC), the Australian Capital Territory (ACT), and Tasmania (TAS).
  • Western Australia (WA): Supports contestable retail natural gas plans (Alinta, Kleenheat, AGL, Origin, Simply Energy) and regulated Synergy/Horizon Power residential electricity tariffs.
  • Northern Territory (NT): Supports regulated Jacana Energy residential electricity tariffs and household bottled LPG gas supplies.

4. Explain My Bill Change formulation

When you scan two consecutive bills for the same household service, GlassSpend disaggregates the dollar difference into distinct physical and commercial drivers:

Total Change = Change in Consumption + Change in Tariff Rate + Change in Supply Charge + Change in Period Length + Change in Credits + Residual

Residual honesty: If a portion of the variance cannot be cleanly itemised (such as unlisted stepped blocks or tax roundings), it is preserved as an unexplained residual rather than falsely attributed.

5. Stay & Save factual negotiation scripts

Instead of pressuring you into risky switches, GlassSpend generates structured negotiation scripts. These scripts cite exact published plan codes, reference price comparisons, and specific unit rate differences, empowering you to negotiate directly with your existing provider to match cheaper rates without switching friction.
