Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 13 October 2026 | Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) Compliant

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

GlassSpend is engineered on the principle that your household financial information belongs exclusively to you. We believe that personal cost intelligence should never come at the cost of your privacy or commercial profiling.

2. What We Collect and How We Process It

2.1 On-Device Document OCR Processing

When you scan a utility bill, broadband statement, or purchase receipt, document character recognition runs entirely on-device using local machine learning. Your raw document photos and image scans never leave your smartphone.

2.2 Candidate Structured Information

Only extracted text candidate fields (such as service provider name, billing cycle, stated dollar amounts, and due dates) are handled by the application. You are always prompted to review, edit, and confirm these values before they are recorded into your local household database.

2.3 What We Strictly Never Collect

  • No bank credentials or passwords: We never ask for your banking passwords, utility portal logins, or PIN numbers.
  • No Consumer Data Right (CDR) banking feeds: We do not monitor or scrape your daily bank transaction history.
  • No sale of personal data: We do not sell, rent, or share personal or financial information with third-party data brokers, insurers, or marketers.
  • No retailer commissions: We do not track you to collect switching referral bounties.

3. On-Device Storage & Local Cryptographic Shredding

Documents processed on-device are stored strictly in private, sandboxed application storage. Temporary scan files in the local app cache are permanently shredded immediately after candidate review and confirmation. No raw bill images or PDFs leave your device.

4. Your Data Rights & Australian Consumer Law

In accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can export all your bill records, notes, and tracking data in standard CSV or JSON format at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit or correct any bill record directly within the mobile application.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete any bill, watch, or your entire account with immediate, unrecoverable local wiping. See our Account & Data Deletion page.
