Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 | Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)

1. Our privacy commitment

GlassSpend is designed on the principle that your household financial information belongs exclusively to you. Personal cost organisation should never come at the expense of your privacy, personal security, or commercial profiling.

2. What information we process

2.1 On-device document processing

When you scan a paper bill or import a digital PDF, optical character recognition runs entirely on-device using local machine learning libraries. Your raw bill photographs and PDF documents are processed in local sandboxed storage and are never uploaded to remote servers for text extraction.

2.2 Candidate structured fields

Extracted values (such as provider names, billing cycles, stated dollar figures, and due dates) are surfaced as candidate fields for your review. You inspect, edit, and confirm these values before they are recorded into your local household database.

2.3 Local-first data storage

Your confirmed bills, payment dates, personal notes, and service watches are stored in a local SQLite database within the sandboxed application environment on your Android device. The application does not require you to create an external consumer account or log in with third-party social credentials.

2.4 Temporary capture files

Temporary image renders and cached capture files generated during the scanning process are deleted from sandboxed application cache immediately upon confirmation or cancellation, with an automated 24-hour purge backstop.

2.5 Authorised cloud assistance

If you choose to use optional cloud-assisted research features, outbound transmissions are restricted to an allowlist of minimised, non-identifying metadata (such as service category, state, 4-digit postcode, general usage tier, and non-identifying plan rate). We strictly reject personal identifiers, customer names, residential street addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, credit card numbers, and 10 or 11 digit meter identifiers (such as National Metering Identifiers or Meter Installation Registration Numbers).

2.6 Google Play subscription verification

If you purchase a GlassSpend Pro subscription, payment processing is handled directly by Google Play. Our backend receives a cryptographic purchase token to verify subscription validity and bind entitlement to an anonymous installation identifier. We do not receive or store your credit card or banking details.

3. What we strictly never collect

  • No bank credentials: We never request, collect, or store your internet banking usernames, passwords, or security codes.
  • No Consumer Data Right (CDR) account feeds: We do not connect to or scrape your transactional bank feeds.
  • No sale of personal data: We do not sell, rent, or trade household information with third-party data brokers, insurers, or marketers.
  • No retailer switching bounties: We do not receive referral fees or commissions for switching your services.

4. Backups and data portability

  • System-managed backups: Standard Android Auto Backup may back up application databases to your personal Google Drive in accordance with operating system settings and our configured backup rules.
  • Manual encrypted export: You can export an encrypted backup file (protected by AES-256-GCM encryption with a password of your choice) or export your records in CSV format directly from Settings.

5. Your data rights

In accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles:

  • Access and portability: You can view and export all your stored bill records at any time.
  • Correction: You can edit any bill record or service detail directly within the mobile application.
  • Deletion: You can clear all local records instantly using the "Clear All Local Data" button in Settings. For web-accessible deletion requests regarding installation tokens or server logs, see our Account & Data Deletion page.
